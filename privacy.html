Privacy Policy

Information about how Saifex applications handle personal information and user data.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

General information

Saifex develops and publishes mobile applications. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when users access or use our applications.

The exact data processed depends on the application and the features used.

Vaktija Slovenija

Vaktija Slovenija provides prayer times and Islamic-related information for users in Slovenia.

Location information

If location-based functionality is enabled, the application may use device location information to determine the user's approximate location and provide relevant prayer times.

Location permission is only used when required for location-related functionality and may be disabled through device settings.

Advertising

The application may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address and interaction information according to their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Notification permissions may be used for prayer reminders and other relevant app functionality.

Učimo se slovenščino

Učimo se slovenščino is an educational application designed to help users learn the Slovenian language.

Learning progress

The application may store information relating to lesson progress and application preferences locally on the user's device.

Advertising

Where advertising is enabled, Google AdMob or other advertising technologies may process limited technical information required for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Modelio

Modelio is a marketplace platform designed to connect people offering beauty, hair and makeup services with people interested in participating as models.

Account information

When creating an account, Modelio may process information such as:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile information
  • Profile photo
  • City or region

User-generated content

Users may submit listings, profile information, messages, photographs, reviews and other content required to use marketplace functionality.

Messages

Messages between users are stored to provide the application's chat functionality.

Location

Approximate location or city information may be used to sort listings and models based on geographical relevance.

Account deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated information through available account settings or by contacting Saifex support.

Third-party services

Saifex applications may use third-party infrastructure and services necessary to provide application functionality.

These may include services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Apple services
  • Supabase
  • Firebase or similar notification services

Third-party providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no digital system or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

Saifex applications are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children unless explicitly stated for a specific application and permitted by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when applications, legal requirements or third-party services change.

The latest version will always be available on this website.
